# Three-minute demonstration

1. **Arrive.** Open the main demo. Point out the two rack faces, the shared stacker aisle and the container waiting at the inbound plate. Identify the vendor by name.
2. **Predict.** Select **Next · Predict**. Read the dwell estimate and expected pickup in GST. Try a different date or change the handling priority. Open **Override position** to choose a rack, row and column. An occupied, unavailable or incompatible cell blocks placement.
3. **Place.** Select **Next · Place container**. The pallet stops at the handoff. The stacker grips and lifts the container, moves down the aisle while raising it, then extends sideways to set it down in the rack. Check the storage and energy counters and select **Next container** when ready.

Open **Engineering** to compare vendor predictions, read about the handling sequence and view the placement-only benchmarks.

To take a slot out of service, open **2D map**, select the slot and choose **Restrict slot**. The demo updates its recommendation and blocks pickup from that slot until you choose **Remove restriction**.
