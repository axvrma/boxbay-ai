# Three-minute demonstration

1. Open the root and point out the 45 stored containers in two racks. The electronics shipment is waiting at inbound.
2. Select **Next · Predict**. Read the dwell estimate and expected pickup in GST. Open model details to show how changing an input from its training median changes the prediction.
3. Set the simulation date to **13 March 2025**. Compare the Ramadan estimate with the prior December result. Select **Previous** to restore the original conditions and recommendation.
4. Select **Next · Place container**. Watch the tray reach the rack and the crane move the shipment. Show the occupancy and energy counters as the move commits.
5. Select **Next · Review optimization**. Inspect the proposed move, estimated pickup benefit and modeled relocation cost. Select **Optimize** to authorize the operation. Use **Conditions** to demonstrate crane outages and show the no-action result when neither crane is online.
6. Open **Engineering** to compare date and congestion inputs side by side, then inspect the matched benchmarks. Several scenarios show higher cost under predictive staging even when mean pickup time improves.
