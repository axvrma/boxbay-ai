# Energy and CO₂e

Per move, each empty or loaded segment accumulates vertical lift energy `m × g × h / efficiency / 3,600,000` kWh. Lowering credit is bounded by the available gravitational potential times the regeneration factor. Horizontal traction scales with distance and load factor. Auxiliary power uses movement time. The total net draw is floored at zero to assume no exported electricity credit. Powered idle uses availability minus occupied time. Interrupted and unfinished moves accrue time-proportional energy. Tray traction is counted on both legs with a distance coefficient and a loaded-mass coefficient. Tray speed and docking affect duration. Acceleration and braking are represented by configurable traction coefficients.

EPA eGRID CAMX 2023 revision 2 gives 429.983 lb CO₂e per MWh, or about 0.19504 kg CO₂e per kWh. The factor is a location-based generation average. Gross grid losses, marginal time-of-day effects, reefer electricity, embodied energy, ship and truck energy are outside the model boundary. Comparisons are between scheduling policies operating the same high-bay system. Use the Engineering multiplier to test sensitivity and telemetry to calibrate coefficients.
