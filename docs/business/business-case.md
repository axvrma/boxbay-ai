# Business case

The experiment tests the incremental scheduling opportunity within a high-bay storage system. The strongest comparison holds the layout and workload constant and replaces only the predictive scoring terms. The benchmark lab exposes metrics across seeds, including cases where anticipation worsens service or raises energy.

The illustrative USD equation is `(baseline kWh − predictive kWh) × energy sensitivity × electricity tariff × equivalent runs − annual cloud cost`. Equivalent runs must correspond to the same workload distribution and scale. The projection excludes labor, throughput revenue and capital savings. Monetize those separately with verified unit costs, sufficient demand, capacity economics and an agreed accounting boundary.

The pilot evidence package should include movement event timestamps, appointment revisions, crane path and power telemetry, rack restrictions and reasons for exceptions. Compare in shadow mode against a strong current-state policy with validated deadlines and safety constraints.
